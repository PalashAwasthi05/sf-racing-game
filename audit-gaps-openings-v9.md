# Gaps and openings audit (v9)

This is a bounded source audit. It contains no location-specific heuristic.

1. **Roof-band facades can be vertically double-offset.** In `work/history/v7/build_world.py:208-210`, a tier gets `elevationOffset=base_roof+band['bottom']-z0`, then `facades.compile` is called with `z0=base_roof+band['bottom']`. `work/pipeline/facades.py:59-64` places every block at `z0+z`; the tier's absolute offset is therefore mixed with a relative one. This can detach wall tops from roof caps or stack them too high. Fix: define one datum contract (compiler `z0` relative to the tier, or tier offset relative to the building) and assert the emitted top against the cap interval. Synthetic regression: a one-tier band with `z0=10`, `bottom=2`, `top=4` must emit wall geometry in `[12,14]` exactly once.

2. **Envelope masks manufacture cracks at wall boundaries.** `work/pipeline/facades.py:25-28` differences each footprint edge by a buffered mask, and `:31` drops any resulting segment shorter than `.3`. A legitimate narrow wall return or aperture-adjacent piece can disappear; the remaining independent blocks do not overlap. Fix: distinguish “masked by an owned envelope” from “short residual,” retain narrow owned wall returns, and use a shared tolerance/overlap policy. Synthetic regression: a 0.12 m wall return beside an envelope mask remains present, while a fully covered edge emits nothing; adjacent wall solids have no gap larger than 1 mm.

3. **Default facade generation always invents a rectangular window grid.** `work/pipeline/facades.py:67-89` computes bays, rows, rectangular opening dimensions, glazing, frames, mullions, transom, sill, and depth cues even when there is no observation. The same unconditional repetition is in `work/pipeline/facade_grammar.py:16-24`, where a missing fit gets default `nx/ny`, ratios, and rectangular `rect`s. Fix: require explicit observed openings or an explicitly opted-in repeat prior per surface; otherwise emit continuous wall. Synthetic regression: empty observations/profile produce zero openings and zero mullion geometry; an opted-in two-by-one repeat produces exactly two.

4. **Opening shape and trim style collapse to rectangles/default divisions.** `work/pipeline/envelopes.py:14-18` defaults every aperture to a rectangle, while `work/pipeline/facade_grammar.py:75-97` adds a default mullion fraction `.25` and transom `.76` to every opening. This explains wrong window shapes and styles even when the wall itself is fitted. Fix: carry `shapeKind`/polygon and division evidence through the opening record; default divisions to empty. Synthetic regression: an arched polygon remains arched after clipping and has no mullion/transom unless supplied; an empty style emits no divisions.

5. **Doors are a visual side effect of the center bay, not an opening type.** `work/pipeline/facades.py:94-102` places a slab, two steel posts, and optional canopy only when `row==0 and col==cols//2`; `work/pipeline/facade_grammar.py:106-107` adds handle-like bars for `entry`/`shopfront` but never builds a door leaf or validates a door boundary. Fix: model `kind='door'`/`entry` as explicit observed openings with leaf, threshold, and swing/portal components; never infer the center bay. Synthetic regression: a three-bay facade with an off-center explicit door creates one door at its supplied UV; with no door evidence, no door hardware or canopy is emitted.

6. **Negative evidence conflates a sign carrier with opaque wall ownership.** `work/pipeline/surface_evidence.py:31-34` treats `sign-carrier` exactly like `solid`/`opaque-panel` and rejects any opening intersecting it. A billboard/sign panel can therefore erase a legitimate opening or force a wall patch to win by overlap. Fix: keep carrier evidence as an occluding overlay with its own depth/owner; it may suppress text/openings only on the carrier footprint, never replace the host wall. Synthetic regression: an opening behind a carrier is retained in the wall record but hidden only by the carrier render layer; an opening outside the carrier remains admissible.

7. **Lettering remaps carrier types without checking a real host component.** `work/pipeline/lettering.py:20-39` maps `opaque-surface`, `glass`, `entrance-canopy`, and `freestanding-canopy` to broad kinds; `:31-35` dereferences a profile/node/grid, while `:36-39` accepts a generated canopy based only on `a.canopy`. This can put billboard text on the wrong surface or float it in space. Fix: resolve a typed carrier ID to an emitted envelope/opening/assembly record, reject missing or ambiguous ownership, and derive all placement from that record. Synthetic regression: each carrier type resolves to one existing component; a missing component raises a deterministic validation error and emits no mesh.

8. **Billboard geometry is emitted after facade/envelope compilation with no occlusion or collision gate.** `work/history/v7/build_world.py:213-216` compiles walls/envelopes and then lettering; `work/pipeline/lettering.py:70-72` unconditionally adds a canopy cube whenever `a.canopy` is present. The billboard slab can penetrate a wall, cap an opening, or intersect a roof. Fix: validate carrier depth against host solids and clip/replace the carrier volume before adding lettering; require a clearance/collision check in the build audit. Synthetic regression: a carrier slab intersecting a wall is clipped to the host plane (or rejected), and a freestanding carrier must remain at least 1 cm clear of all building solids.
