# Market Street corridor

Area revision `746bb73be90c` runs from the Embarcadero plaza to the far end of the Flood Building's Market frontage. The connected route measures 1,609.34 m. The first 64.72 m is a mapped walking and cycling connection. The car starts at Market and Steuart for the remaining 1,544.62 m. This private simulation includes transit and bicycle carriageways.

The area includes the first row of blocks on both sides of Market, 10 northwest and 11 southeast. Selection samples the mapped route every 5 m along perpendicular rays, retains blocks with at least 25 m of frontage and includes the terminal property's full block. Whole building footprints and a 25 m perimeter provide edge context. See `aoi-repair-proof.json`, `selected-blocks.geojson` and `route-drive.geojson`. Coordinates use WGS84, with a local EPSG:32610 frame centered at −122.4015083, 37.7891435.

City and OSM records were retrieved November 16, 2026. The context contains 240 municipal building records, 849 street-tree records and 1,879 OSM ways. The 2023 USGS survey contributed 15,024,329 laser returns. Source dates vary. The street-photo pass considered 713 panoramas. Two-view agreement supplied material evidence for 90 wall facings on 73 source buildings. The separate opening pass examined 1,340 wall portions. It found two usable camera positions for 381, registered 151 pairs, and retained 656 disjoint opening outlines on 35 portions of 26 buildings. Two views supported interior mullions or transoms in 234 of those openings. Other apertures remain inferred. The [source-pair review sheet](imagery/frontage-details.jpg) shows examples at five levels of opening count. Absolute camera poses, some neighboring wall boundaries and glazing materials still carry uncertainty.

From the project root, use `work/geoenv/bin/python` for all commands. Acquire city and OSM inputs with `work/areas/acquire_corridor.py work/areas/market-street`. See [LiDAR acquisition](LIDAR.md) for the cloud. The reusable frontage scripts in the parent folder consume local Mapillary panorama metadata and imagery. Area-specific geography lives in `corridor-source.json` and `config.json`.

Recreate the facade packet from the original material readings with `PYTHONPATH=work work/geoenv/bin/python work/areas/refine_paired_openings.py work/areas/market-street work/areas/market-street/imagery/frontage-profiles.json work/areas/market-street/imagery/frontage-details.json`. Build from those inputs with `work/areas/build_fresh_area.py --area work/areas/market-street --label market-street --memory-gb 6.4 --profiles work/areas/market-street/imagery/frontage-details.json`. Package the drivable scene with `work/areas/package_area_drive.py work/areas/market-street work/king-street/public market-street --gpu-textures`. The frozen spatial index is published through the area's own comparison manifest.
