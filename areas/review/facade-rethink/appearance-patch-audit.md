# Evidence-derived facade appearance audit

## Finding

The missing stage is a source-image-derived, per-surface appearance atlas. Current output has a measured wall frame and UV coordinates, but material appearance is mostly typed color plus generic scan/procedural texture. There is no flow that binds a masked region of a registered facade photograph to the corresponding wall UVs, reconciles lighting across views, or records held-out image agreement. That gap explains why adding more descriptive material tokens alone will keep yielding generic-looking relief and finishes.

## Existing pieces to reuse

- `regen/sides.py` selects registered cameras, rectifies each wall plane, and writes both rectified and perspective crops. `auto_read_frontages_v24.py::rectify` samples the panorama onto metric wall coordinates. `regen/reading_provenance.py` carries source IDs and crop/view provenance; `regen/assemble.py`/reading provenance retain hashes. This is the strongest starting point for repeatable image-to-wall mapping.
- `facade_segment_v27.py` has local SegFormer ADE20K labels and detector proposals for facade parts and common occluders. `mask_facade_obstructions_v12.py` emits diagnostic facade-eligible, opening-candidate, and obstruction masks. It explicitly reports `worldRegistrationValidated:false`, `facadeMeasurementsGenerated:0`, and `texturesPublished:false`; masks are hypotheses, not production-ready texel ownership.
- `regen/colours.py` estimates zone colors from rectified crops and reconciles hue/chroma across views. `panorama_balance.py::road_gain` makes a panorama-level neutral-road white-balance correction. Neither estimates per-pixel albedo or removes directional shadows/specular effects.
- `pipeline/mesh_attributes.py`, `pipeline/bays.py`, and `generate_detailed_v14.py` preserve/consume metric UVs. `apply_materials_v13.py` can swap material maps on UV-bearing meshes while preserving geometry. `general_world/material_synthesis.py` creates deterministic metric color, normal, and roughness maps; `general_world/scanned_surface_variant.py` validates/recolors generic scans. These establish a usable material-coordinate and map-emission path, but their textures are generic rather than samples of the observed surface.
- `apply_textures_v10.py` reuses generic scanned assets where recipes match; its report marks `imageVerifiedClassification:false`. v13 likewise reports `imageVerifiedAssignment:false` and calls its inputs historical descriptors, not photo-derived classification. Its scans have source/license metadata for those assets, but this does not cover derived Mapillary crops.

## Pilot evidence and limits

The calibrated review set has 22 source jobs, 44 rectified crops, and 44 perspective context crops. Twenty jobs are marked registered; two remain `bias`. Rectification is resampled at up to 45 px/m, so it must not be mistaken for native detail. At the selected camera distances, estimated panorama sampling is about 18–126 source px/m (median about 50 px/m): broad siding courses can be resolved in nearer views, but fine mortar and small carved relief are subpixel or marginal for much of the set. Crop sightline metadata is useful for ranking, not a guarantee of unobstructed pixels; notes record trees, trucks, parked cars, flare, and hedges. Keep source masks and per-zone valid-pixel coverage in the experiment, and exclude low-coverage zones.

There is a valuable generic Mapillary mesh/SfM diagnostic cache, but it does not solve appearance mapping. `imagery-street-v12/entities` contains 1,111 entity JSONs and `clusters` 70 cluster files; `imagery-facade-v12` has 160 entity and 69 cluster files. `mapillary_mesh_probe_v12.py` decoded three selected per-capture mesh protobufs in `mapillary-mesh-probe-v12/` and `mapillary-mesh-probe-final-v12/`. They are triangle-indexed camera-frame geometry (field 1 floats, field 2 face indices), not textured meshes: the report has no UV/color channels or depth sampling pitch, and explicitly has `worldRegistration:false` and unverified photo/facade accuracy. The three samples have 2,100–2,519 vertices and 4,062–4,876 triangles, with median triangle edges 1.1–1.8 m and p90 edges 10.7–36.3 m. This may help coarse camera-space occlusion where correspondence is established; it is far too coarse and unregistered to transfer trim texture. No new data was fetched for this audit.

Local source records preserve Mapillary URLs, image hashes, capture dates, and view groups, but no license field was found. This is enough to maintain traceability in a private experiment, not to establish publication rights for derived image patches; check the source terms before distributing atlases.

## Smallest useful experiment

Use two distinct, well-covered measured zones from different facades, each with two registered views: one brick face and one painted/render or timber face. For each, use the existing rectified crop and wall frame, combine the geometry-derived opening masks with the diagnostic semantic mask, and manually QA only the mask boundary/occluders for the pilot. Build a wall-coordinate RGB patch from view A after robust low-frequency exposure normalization; use view B only as held-out evidence. Measure valid facade coverage, cross-view color residual (e.g. median ΔE on mutually visible pixels), and image reprojection residual before baking the patch to the material UV. Keep coarse pigment and high-frequency pattern as separate outputs: a low-frequency color field can follow the measured finish, while the high-frequency patch/period estimate carries brick joints, board grain, or relief. Compare against the current generic PBR material in the held-out view, and report failures for shadowed, specular, or occluded zones rather than filling them synthetically. This tests both reusable mapping and appearance consistency without turning one facade into a rule.

Before scaling to all 22, require two-source success and a held-out registered view for each sample; preserve source IDs/hashes, transforms, mask coverage, and rights status with every patch. The current crops are sufficient to test broad visible texture on selected facades, but not to promise reliable fine trim recovery or complete clean coverage across the full cohort.
