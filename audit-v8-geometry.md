# Geometry audit v8

Bounded read-only review of `work/build_world.py` and the requested pipeline modules. No named-building data was inspected. Each test below is location-free: it uses synthetic geometry or captured compiler output rather than a particular building.

## Findings

1. **Envelope reference lines are trusted without footprint validation (certain source bug).** `work/pipeline/envelopes.py:43-57` accepts `profile['referenceLine']` and derives `left`, `right`, and `plane` without checking that the line lies on, or even near, `c['g']`. `replacement_mask()` then uses that frame at `:150-155`, and `compile()` emits the envelope from it at `:158-224`. A stale or misregistered line therefore creates floating envelope walls, windows, and assemblies outside the building footprint. Correction: require a reference line to overlap a compatible footprint edge within a bounded distance and clip its interval to one contiguous edge; reject the profile when that check fails. Regression: pass a square footprint and a parallel reference line several metres away; preparation/compilation must reject it or emit zero envelope vertices outside the footprint.

2. **Fallback frontage frames bridge separate parallel edges (certain source bug).** In `envelopes.py:48-57`, all edges parallel to the requested tangent whose plane is within `planeToleranceM` are collected, then one global min/max of their projected points becomes the frontage. Disjoint edges, courtyard edges, or two setbacks can therefore become one continuous rectangle, producing openings across a gap or across a corner. Correction: choose one connected edge chain and split frames at every gap/corner; validate the resulting span against the footprint. Regression: use a footprint with two separated collinear frontage segments inside the plane tolerance and assert every frame interval is covered by a connected footprint edge, with no interval spanning the gap.

3. **Corner joins can be applied twice when observations encode both directions (conditional source bug).** `envelopes.prepare()` iterates every profile's `joins` at `:115-132`, updates both frames, and records the event on both profiles. If A links B and B links A, the same corner adjustment is performed twice, changing `left`/`width` a second time and leaving loose returns or misaligned adjacent surfaces. The source logic has no pair de-duplication; whether this triggers depends on profile data. Correction: canonicalize each undirected profile/link pair and solve it once. Regression: create two reciprocal links with a known corner; assert one adjustment per pair, unchanged on a second prepare, and matching corner endpoints.

4. **Generic façade windows are emitted on every eligible footprint ring, including unobserved or courtyard edges (unjustified inference; suspected visual cause).** `facades.compile()` iterates every exterior and interior ring at `:23-29`. For each segment that passes the broad `visible` test at `:57`, it synthesizes repeated openings, frames, sills, and entries through `:65-103`, even when no observation or street-facing evidence exists. This can look like random walls and windows over a void or on a side that should remain unresolved. Correction: gate repeated openings by a verified frontage/observation, and use an opaque wall or explicit unresolved surface for unsupported edges; treat interior rings separately. Regression: compile a polygon with an interior hole and no observation; assert unsupported ring segments have no synthesized window assemblies and that all emitted opening polygons are contained by an allowed frontage mask.

5. **Roof-band morphology can manufacture connected/floating roof regions (unjustified inference; suspected visual cause).** `spatial_rules.roof_bands()` converts a raster mask to polygons and applies `.buffer(.45).buffer(-.45).simplify(.2)` at `:45-55`. This closes sub-metre gaps and smooths boundaries without checking that the resulting region remains a measured roof component. `build_world.py:200-211` then caps and compiles façade tiers from those regions. Correction: preserve connected components from the measured mask, clip every band to the supporting footprint/roof sample support, and use simplification that cannot cross gaps. Regression: feed two roof islands separated by a narrow low strip; assert output remains two components and every cap polygon is covered by the input footprint and support mask.

6. **Mapped stairs infer every riser from only two endpoint terrain samples (unjustified inference; suspected visual cause).** `mobility.compile()` samples `za`/`zb` only at `g.coords[0]` and `g.coords[-1]` (`:56-64`), then assigns linear step heights while each cuboid is placed on the path. A bent or locally uneven path can consequently bury some steps in terrain or leave others floating; the handrail at `:65-70` follows sampled terrain rather than the generated tread tops. Correction: sample terrain per riser interval, construct treads against local support, and emit unresolved stairs when support/rise is inconsistent. Regression: use a path whose midpoint terrain is above the endpoint interpolation; assert each tread top is supported by local terrain and handrail endpoints coincide with tread-top elevations.

7. **Sign deduplication is a coarse global 7 m grid (certain source/inference bug).** `streets.compile()` rounds every inventory point to `(round(x/7), round(y/7), family)` at `:75-85`. Two valid same-family signs within one grid cell, even on different curbs or approaches, collapse to one panel; the surviving sign's curb snap and orientation then represent the wrong source. Correction: deduplicate only when source points, snapped curb component/side, and orientation are all within explicit small tolerances. Regression: provide two same-family points 6 m apart on separate synthetic curb segments and assert two sign panels/records are emitted.

8. **Tree leaves are unconstrained procedural blades, so they need not attach to a branch or avoid scene geometry (unjustified inference; suspected loose-object cause).** `vegetation.compile()` places deciduous leaf centers from an unconstrained 3-D random offset at `:31-34`; it does not test those blades against the tree's branch segments, buildings, or other occluders. The deterministic seed makes the result repeatable but does not make the geometry supported. Correction: generate leaves from branch-local frames and reject/clip blades that intersect building volumes or fall below terrain. Regression: capture `bark` and `leaf*` meshes for a synthetic tree beside a wall; assert every leaf base is within a small distance of a branch segment and no leaf triangle intersects the wall volume.

## Classification

Findings 1, 2, and 7 are direct source-code defects independent of the named-building data. Finding 3 is a direct algorithmic defect when reciprocal links are present. Findings 4–6 and 8 are explicit inference mechanisms whose visual impact depends on the input geometry; they are the likely causes of the reported random walls, unsupported surfaces, inaccurate stairs, and loose objects when the scene contains the corresponding topology.
