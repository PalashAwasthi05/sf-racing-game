**Revision 0.7: whole-area ownership, physical support and broad evidence coverage**

Every substantial compiler change requires a full study-area regeneration. A correction to one photographed building is insufficient evidence that the heuristic improved. The release records the compiler/input fingerprint, complete building set, physical checks and unresolved observation queue. An unchanged old mesh cannot silently survive a rule revision.

The new observation pass covers twelve additional frontages: 128 King; 180, 200, 224, 139, 133/135 and 101 Townsend; both China Basin buildings; 25 and 45/47 Lusk; and the low Third Street shop row. The source packet separates literal observations from estimated metric layout. These remain data records consumed by the same compiler, not address-specific rendering branches. Some long-frontage bay counts and cropped-region bounds remain estimates; those are not promoted to measured observations.

1. **Resolve physical ownership before appearance.** Reconcile sufficiently agreeing mapped outlines with municipal identities, then remove overlapping ownership globally. Source edit timestamps are retained, but are not construction or capture dates. A new lease or directory name cannot demolish an existing footprint. Known tenant changes invalidate earlier lettering without supplying new, unobserved sign text. Ambiguous replacement buildings remain a source-fusion problem needing dated construction evidence.
2. **Support every footprint edge.** Sample terrain around every exterior and courtyard boundary. A foundation extends beneath the lowest boundary sample up to the building's structural datum; the building's measured roof heights are preserved. Curbs follow terrain with short supported faces. These checks eliminate an identified mechanism for air gaps; they do not establish that the ground model or entrance threshold is surveyed accurately.
3. **Give every wall and roof interval one owner.** Compile LiDAR roof tiers as nested superlevel volumes with disjoint height intervals and exposed caps. Roof risers are opaque unless independent facade observations supply apertures; rooftop height changes are not automatic window floors. Adjacent facade patches produce only the exposed difference in depth, avoiding two buried return walls on the same boundary. Replacement masks split original wall edges before rebuilding them. Remove coincident triangles after batching.
4. **Represent the whole photographed frontage as regions.** Base, upper bands, projecting window stacks, solid piers, glazed additions, arches, crowns and sign carriers have independent bounds and materials. Windows never gain authority from a nearby building's style or a different material zone. A material observed on one face is not automatically copied over all unobserved sides. Arch geometry clips its mullions to the true aperture. Open fire escapes, attached steps, pilasters and cornice profiles are shared geometry operators triggered by evidence, with estimated dimensions explicitly retained.
5. **Generate street details from mobility topology.** Parse both sides of lane and parking tags, including asymmetric widths. Separately mapped cycleways own their surfaces; shared promenades do not automatically become protected bicycle lanes. Mapped steps use step counts and terrain rise where available; unresolved rise is queued. Railings, walls and bollards require mapped barrier evidence, and handrails require explicit evidence. Tram-track proximity supplies none. Paint width, cadence and uncertain curb offsets remain inferred until checked against imagery or surveyed plans.
6. **Track freshness per claim.** Maintain independent photo-capture, publication, OSM-edit and confirmed-change fields. Missing capture dates stay unknown. A capture within the 24-month budget is only recent enough for this prototype, not proof that the building is unchanged today. Current tenant text needs visual evidence of its actual carrier. In this pass, the photographed former BASEBALLISM name is excluded after 2026 change evidence; no uninspected CardVault wording is synthesized. Old physical FIRST REPUBLIC BANK lettering remains explicitly tied to its May 2025 photograph.
7. **Allocate review across the map.** Rank missing or stale wall observations by approximate driver-visible area, distance and occlusion. Count unique buildings with direct evidence, unobserved visible sections and stale/unknown capture dates. Do not use window or triangle count as realism progress. Audit every generated identity for foundation support and source ownership, then evaluate real resemblance from held-out street views. Failing a physical check or a current-source requirement blocks an accuracy claim.

**What runs automatically now:** source-outline reconciliation, foundation support sampling, roof volume construction, wall ownership, deterministic component expansion, material-family resolution, literal text attachment, dated-source classification, mapped mobility parsing, geometry validation and whole-scene rebuilding. The facade graphs in this revision were still interpreted and annotated from photos by the assistant. Dense image segmentation, calibrated multiview reconstruction and independently measured street resemblance are not automated or validated. The compiler can catch contradictions represented in its evidence; it cannot discover every missing real feature without acquiring that evidence.

**Low-token scaling requirement:** acquire and annotate a streetscape capture once, assign its regions to multiple buildings, and cache the compact graph and source dates. Repeated members expand numerically with no model tokens. Preserve exception masks and material boundaries in that graph. A source or rule change invalidates affected outputs; a major revision rebuilds the whole benchmark. Measure capture selection, annotation minutes, correction passes and token cost separately from numerical generation. This pass does not prove the one-person-month San Francisco target.

The broader packet includes dated Google Street View captures from January, March and May 2025 and Lusk in January 2023, plus undated actual owner/architect photographs. All source photograph pixels remain research-only; they are not included in the model, Site or downloadable bundle. The visible-source ledger is distributed with the heuristic.
