**Revision 0.3: detail is a reconstruction layer**

The first pilot demonstrated placement and approximate massing, but generic window grids and flat colors did not meet the supplied game reference. That failure changes the completion criteria: a populated mesh is not a finished environment. Facade layout, depth, surface response, street edges, signs, and evidence coverage must each pass their own checks.

The reference image defines a perceptual target: convincing material grain at driving distance; reveals, frames, glazing divisions, sills, canopies and roof edges with physical depth; plausible vegetation silhouettes; readable, correctly posed signs; curb transitions and road paint; consistent daylight and contact shadows. It does not supply evidence of San Francisco's architecture. Its buildings or street furniture must not be copied into this city.

The revised unit of work is an **exposed wall section or street segment**, with structured observations as input. An address can identify a source record, but must never select custom geometry inside the compiler. The same record schema feeds the same components everywhere. Architectural priors remain explicit hypotheses when observations are absent.

1. **Measure coverage before decorating.** Enumerate exposed walls from footprints and roof tiers, including courtyard and upper setback walls. Enumerate street edges and attachment sites. Create missing-observation jobs before generating a convincing fallback. Camera selection should maximize newly visible uncertain area per capture, accounting for obliqueness, occlusion, date and resolution. One street panorama can serve several buildings; do not issue one language-model call per window.
2. **Extract an observation packet once.** Where access permits, store the capture date and camera pose, wall corners, floor bands, opening rectangles, mullion topology, material labels, protrusion evidence, attachments and unknowns. Use source images and geometry registration rather than prose descriptions as the durable input. Batch vision can propose labels and rectangles; deterministic fitting supplies dimensions and residuals. Cache the compact packet by source ID/content hash and extractor version.
3. **Fit a compact facade description.** Solve wall-local coordinates, floor levels, opening positions and sizes, frame types, projection/recess depth, sill dimensions, ground-floor use, and roof transitions. Repeated elements share parameters; irregular exceptions are data. A single image can support visible opening shape, but a depth estimate remains inferred without another view or depth measurement. Never turn a guessed column count into an observed count.
4. **Compile actual architectural depth.** Create walls around openings, with recessed glazing, jambs, mullions, transoms, sills and canopies. Apply the compiler to every exposed vertical level, including roof setbacks. Opaque massing behind a transparent-looking window is not an adequate substitute for a reveal. Large-scale silhouette and shadow-producing features require geometry; tiny mortar or aggregate detail belongs in material maps.
5. **Separate material identity from material quality.** Prefer licensed, rectified surface observations for the actual wall when available. Use reusable scanned PBR maps for the correct material class otherwise, marking the selection as inferred. Keep albedo, normal, roughness and physical tile scale separate. Generated or procedural weathering can add appearance variation but cannot establish the real location of stains, cracks, signs or windows. Do not generate an entire facade image and call its invented openings accurate.
6. **Construct the street edge.** Recover road and sidewalk boundaries, curb height, crossfalls, curb ramps, tactile paving, expansion joints, pits and drains as connected geometry. ROW polygons constrain plausible extent but are not measured curb lines. Lane arrows and signs depend on traffic semantics and current installed evidence. Coarse inventory points can seed searches; a curb snap is an inferred placement with a recorded displacement, not a survey result.
7. **Compile reusable objects with traceable poses.** Sign content comes from a corroborated legend/OCR record; position, direction and dimensions have independent evidence fields. Trees use observed placement and supported species/canopy information; individual branches remain synthetic unless measured. Utility wires follow observed anchors and a fitted sag model; arbitrary wires should not be added simply to imitate the visual reference.
8. **Validate both likeness and evidence.** Compare held-out street views at matched poses, inspect close range, and measure projected edge/opening error, silhouette difference, curb and sign displacement, material mismatch, temporal age and observation coverage. Increased triangle count, texture resolution or shadow quality cannot satisfy the correspondence tests. An attractive fallback must still fail the accuracy gate.

Suggested pilot acceptance targets—not validated guarantees—are at least 95% of street-visible wall area covered by usable dated observations, two sufficiently separated views for depth-bearing features, opening and curb positions checked to 0.15 m, and sign positions checked to 0.30 m with orientation and legend corroborated. Hidden walls and occluded features remain explicit gaps. The exact tolerances should be tuned against the reference and driving-camera distances, then held fixed for the citywide estimate.

**What revision 0.3 actually implements**

The second King Street compiler uses a region configuration, separate facade observation records, semantic priors, shared facade components, per-material geometry batches, scanned brick/asphalt/concrete at metric UV scale, row-aware window assemblies, tier-wall treatment, a street-detail compiler, sign inventory candidates with uncertainty, and species-based foliage geometry. It emits evidence records and an ordered missing-view queue. Regeneration from cached geospatial data uses no language-model calls. The browser downloads compressed bounded-size chunks of the compiled model.

The implemented facade observations are sparse visual fits from two January 2025 Street View viewpoints. They were recorded during this session; automated bulk facade extraction, multi-view metric calibration and held-out image scoring are **not implemented or validated**. A Mapillary metadata adapter and observation schema are supplied, but the adapter has not been run with authorized account credentials. Scanned material maps are reusable CC0 examples, not captures of the actual walls. Most generated facade sections therefore remain unverified priors.

The current export bakes many repeated components into ordinary glTF meshes. Transfer chunks are not spatial streaming or HLOD. Production-scale instancing, shared texture residency, distance-dependent detail and engine-side collision/streaming still need a measured implementation before extrapolating to all San Francisco. Record generation time, raw and transfer size, triangles, draw calls, memory and frame time alongside accuracy; faster loading alone does not reduce geometric cost.

**Preventing location-specific fixes**

Keep source adapters, observation records, fitting, component generation and rendering separate. There must be no address ID, landmark name or target coordinate in a geometry-selection branch. Geographic identifiers belong in input matching. Regional road standards and architecture priors are configuration with provenance. A correction should either add a reusable component, improve a shared fit, fix source reconciliation, or supply new evidence through the existing schema.

Test portability by changing the region and using a withheld neighborhood with no new compiler branches. A system that needs manual facade coding for each new block fails the one-month target even if this pilot looks good. The appropriate citywide estimate is observed correction minutes per wall/street segment plus automated processing and data-acquisition time—not tokens spent to produce one polished image.
